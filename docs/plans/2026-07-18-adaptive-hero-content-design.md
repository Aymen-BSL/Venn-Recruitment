# Adaptive Hero Content Design

The hero content will no longer depend on viewport-based top padding. Its container keeps the full `100svh` height, reserves the 5rem header area with top padding, and centers the complete content group inside the remaining space.

Headline sizing will consider both viewport width and viewport height. Existing short-screen rules continue to reduce copy spacing, button height, and trust-line spacing where necessary. This keeps the composition balanced on tall displays while preventing crowding on short laptops and mobile screens.
